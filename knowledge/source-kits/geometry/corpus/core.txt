point: location in space with no size
line: straight one-dimensional figure extending without end
triangle: polygon with three sides and three angles
circle: set of points at fixed distance radius from a center
Pythagorean theorem: in a right triangle a^2 + b^2 = c^2
triangle angle sum: interior angles sum to 180 degrees in Euclidean geometry
circumference: C = 2*pi*r
circle area: A = pi*r^2
Sources: Euclid Elements (public domain) + standard open geometry curricula.
