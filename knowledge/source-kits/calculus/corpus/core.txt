limit: value a function approaches as the input approaches a point
derivative: instantaneous rate of change as limit of [f(x+h)-f(x)]/h
integral: accumulation; antiderivative linked by the fundamental theorem of calculus
power rule: d/dx x^n = n x^(n-1)
sum rule: derivative of sum is sum of derivatives
chain rule: d/dx f(g(x)) = f'(g(x)) g'(x)
fundamental theorem: derivative of integral from a to x of f is f(x)
