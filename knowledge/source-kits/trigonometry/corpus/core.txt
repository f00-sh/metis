sine: opposite over hypotenuse in a right triangle
cosine: adjacent over hypotenuse
tangent: sine over cosine
Pythagorean identity: sin^2 θ + cos^2 θ = 1
angle sum sine: sin(a+b) = sin a cos b + cos a sin b
angle sum cosine: cos(a+b) = cos a cos b - sin a sin b
double angle sine: sin(2θ) = 2 sin θ cos θ
