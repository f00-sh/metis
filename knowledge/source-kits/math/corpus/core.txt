natural-number: positive whole counting number 1,2,3,...
integer: whole number that may be positive, negative, or zero
rational-number: number expressible as ratio of integers p/q with q≠0
pemdas: parentheses exponents multiplication/division addition/subtraction
additive-identity: a + 0 = a
multiplicative-identity: a * 1 = a
commutative addition: a + b = b + a
distributive law: a*(b+c) = a*b + a*c
Open educational note: arithmetic foundations as taught in elementary algebra courses (OpenStax).
