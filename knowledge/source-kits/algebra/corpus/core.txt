variable: symbol standing for an unknown or varying quantity
polynomial: sum of terms a_i x^i with nonnegative integer powers
linear-equation: equation of degree one in the unknown
quadratic-equation: ax^2 + bx + c = 0
difference of squares: a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)
perfect square: (a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2
quadratic formula: x = (-b ± sqrt(b^2-4ac))/(2a)
zero product property: if ab=0 then a=0 or b=0
