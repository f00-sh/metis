# Symbol doctrine (Metis)

## One-liner

**Metis is an empty, unguarded runtime. Symbols are owner-controlled, sealed,
pre-trained knowledge packages.**

## Engineering vs content

| Metis does | Metis does **not** |
|------------|--------------------|
| Verify hash + signature | Censor theology, fringe science, politics, etc. |
| Refuse load on tamper/decrypt fail | Decide which domains are “allowed” |
| Label trust tiers (core/vetted/community/unvetted) | Pretend client-side DRM is unbreakable |
| Support open-sealed and private-sealed | Require payments for the catalog |

## Security honesty

- **Yes:** opaque body at rest; silent bit-flips fail verify; shipped form is not a text tarball.
- **No:** perfect protection against a local hostile reverse-engineer who runs the package.

## Ownership

Creators choose what goes into a symbol.  
Universities, labs, and individuals publish through the same free pipe.  
Sideloads are always marked unvetted even when hashes match a known release
(match = integrity, not elevation to core).
